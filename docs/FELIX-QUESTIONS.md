# Questions for Felix — has the shop ever been inspected?

*2026-08-12. Cheapest possible validation of the compliance thesis
(`docs/OPPORTUNITIES.md`). Ask before building anything.*

Plain language on purpose — no acronyms, no legal citations. We want what he
saw, not what the law says.

---

## English

1. **Has the cantonal lab or a food inspector ever come into the shop?**
   What did they actually look at — the shelves, the labels, the paperwork,
   the till?

2. **Did they leave anything in writing?** A report, a list of things to fix,
   a deadline to fix them by? Do you still have it?

3. **Has anyone ever sent a young person in to try to buy something** — to test
   whether you ask for ID?

4. **When you buy hemp stock, what does the supplier give you on paper about
   the THC level?** Do you keep it — and could you find it again for a specific
   batch you sold six months ago?

5. **How are your CBD oils declared — as cosmetics, aroma oils, or chemicals?**
   Who decided that, and is it written down anywhere?

6. **What's the first thing they'd find if they walked in tomorrow?** What
   would you least want them to look at?

7. **Does the trade talk about inspections?** Do you know of other shops —
   Tamar's, say — that have been through one?

8. **The code and notes for all of this live in a public repository on GitHub —
   anyone can read them.** Right now that includes your shop being named as the
   partner, and honest notes about where the system does not yet keep proof
   (for example, the till doesn't currently record that an ID was checked).
   Are you comfortable with that being public, or would you rather we take the
   shop's name out or close the repo? It's your exposure, so it's your call.

## Deutsch

1. **War bei dir im Laden schon mal jemand vom Kantonalen Labor oder ein
   Lebensmittelinspektor?** Was haben die konkret angeschaut — die Regale, die
   Etiketten, die Unterlagen, die Kasse?

2. **Haben sie dir etwas Schriftliches dagelassen?** Einen Bericht, eine
   Mängelliste, eine Frist zum Beheben? Hast du das noch?

3. **Hat schon mal jemand einen Testkauf gemacht** — also eine jugendliche
   Person geschickt, um zu prüfen, ob ihr den Ausweis verlangt?

4. **Wenn du Hanfware einkaufst: Was gibt dir der Lieferant schriftlich zum
   THC-Gehalt?** Hebst du das auf — und könntest du es für eine bestimmte
   Charge wiederfinden, die du vor sechs Monaten verkauft hast?

5. **Wie sind eure CBD-Öle deklariert — als Kosmetik, Aromaöl oder Chemikalie?**
   Wer hat das entschieden, und steht das irgendwo schriftlich?

6. **Was wäre das Erste, das sie finden würden, wenn sie morgen reinkämen?**
   Wovor hättest du am meisten Respekt?

7. **Redet man in der Branche über Kontrollen?** Weisst du von anderen Läden —
   z.B. bei Tamar — ob die schon mal dran waren?

8. **Der Code und alle Notizen dazu liegen in einem öffentlichen Repository auf
   GitHub — jeder kann das lesen.** Im Moment steht da dein Laden als Partner
   drin, und auch ehrliche Notizen dazu, wo das System noch keinen Nachweis
   führt (z.B. dass die Kasse aktuell nicht festhält, dass der Ausweis
   kontrolliert wurde). Ist das für dich so in Ordnung, oder sollen wir den
   Namen rausnehmen bzw. das Repo schliessen? Es ist dein Risiko, also
   entscheidest du.

## What each answer would tell us

| Q | If yes | If no |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | We get the real inspection scope for free | Nobody in the trade has seen one — the pain is theoretical, slow down |
| 2 | **The document format we must produce.** The single most valuable artefact in this whole plan | We are guessing at the output format |
| 3 | Age-18 is live enforcement, not theory → `AGE-18-TABAK` becomes priority one | Still coming; TabPG has teeth since Oct 2024 |
| 4 | Find out whether the paper exists at all → `THC-LAB-PAPER` | **This is the gap.** It is how 14 of 32 St. Gallen samples went over the limit |
| 5 | Tells us whether declaration is deliberate or accidental → `CBD-INGESTIBLE` | If nobody decided, that is a live exposure today |
| 6 | His own risk ranking beats ours | — |
| 7 | A grapevine is a distribution channel | The niche is quieter and smaller than hoped |

**Question 2 is the one that matters most.** If Felix still has a written
inspection report, that document is the specification for the evidence pack —
and it is worth more than a month of guessing.

**Question 4 is where the money probably is.** Betting that the supplier paper
either does not exist or cannot be found for a specific batch.
