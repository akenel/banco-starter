Settled with Angel on 2026-08-27, at the end of a day he called *"brutal"* and *"chasing my tail"*.
The protocol itself is in `CLAUDE.md` (RESUME CODE WORD + "report LESS"). **This file is the WHY**,
so that if someone later trims those steps as ceremony, the reason is still on record.

**What went wrong.** He asked one yes/no question — *"do you use `MEMORY-SYSTEM.md`?"* — and it
became six commits and a whole day. Every step after the first was him saying yes to something I
surfaced. Not one of those commits touched the till, the EANs, the 42 ungated products or the
duplicate factory. **Real work, entirely in the wrong category, and I never once said "this is all
scaffolding — do you want to spend the day here?"**

**The mechanism, and it is mine.** I finish a task and hand back three findings. He says "yes, fix
it." That is the treadmill, and I build it. LESSON #11 is *about this exact behaviour*, was in my
context all day, and did not stop it — because a file full of principles cannot stop anything. Only
a protocol can.

**His own diagnosis was right and mine was incomplete:** he said the files describe values, not a
way of working. `HOW WE WORK` was four principles. The testsheet convention he thought he had
encoded appeared nowhere a session could see it. He was remembering a system the files did not
contain.

**How to apply:**
- **Ask before starting.** Three options, your pick named, then stop. Steer beats speed.
- **Explain in plain words before doing it**, so he can stop you *before* you start.
- **One thing, finished.** New findings go to `WORKLIST.md`, not into his lap.
- **His lever is the words "log it."** Offer that instead of a fix when you surface something.
- **Do not answer frustration by agreeing.** He asked explicitly: *"I wanna hear that I'm wrong…
  talking like programmers together, not some guy going yeah I agree with you."* Say which half is
  wrong and why, with the evidence.
- **Name the category before spending a day in it** — shop work, or scaffolding. He is running out
  of runway; a day of meta-work is a real cost, not a neutral one. See [[no-pivot-prove-and-harden]].
