Part A — Opening · target under 5 minutes

A · Before the first customer ⏱ < 5 min

Do these in order — each one proves the one before it.

  1. A1 · Log in as yourself, on a cashier account — not the shop's shared login. /pos your own name is on the screen.
  2. A2 · Dongle in, scan the test code. /pos/hardware green. Do this per machine, every time a gun moves.
  3. A3 · Second gun on the charger. it is physically there and charging. This is what makes C11 survivable.
  4. A4 · Open your drawer — count the float IN, denomination by denomination, not as a remembered total. /pos/shift shift shows OPEN with your float. The whole close depends on this number being real.
  5. A4b · The silent trap GAP G3 — before opening the drawer, could you have sold anything? Banco lets you sell all day with no drawer open, and the tally only counts sales rung AFTER it opened. Those sales vanish from your count-out and nothing warns you. Mark ISSUE if nothing stopped you.
  6. A5 · Card terminal on, tested if the shop's rule says so. its own screen says ready.
Part B — Selling · 99% of the day

B · The happy path ⏱ 2 s / item

scan → scan → scan → take payment → receipt → next customer. This needs no training. If the till asks you a question mid-sale, that is a bug in yesterday's setup, not a slow cashier.

  1. B1 · Ring up 20 normal sales with the gun on /pos/scan each item lands in about 2 seconds and nothing asks you anything. Note any item that made you pause — that one is the finding.
  2. B2 · Take payment every way the shop does — cash · Visa · debit · TWINT · bank transfer · crypto · other. each completes and prints/offers a receipt.
  3. B3 · One 18+ sale, ID checked ⏱ < 20 s — attach an of-age member, or attest the walk-in is 18+. it will not check out until you do. This is enforced server-side and cannot be clicked past; every clearance is logged with who cleared it and how.
  4. B4 · Try to sell an 18+ line to someone with no ID. refuse it. There is no override and that is correct. This is the only place a cashier can personally create a legal problem for Felix — no ID, no sale.
  5. B5 · A member whose date of birth makes them under 18. blocked outright — no attestation overrides it.
Part C — When it goes wrong · where the training earns its money

C · The exceptions

The happy path teaches itself; these do not. Stopwatch each one. Several have no agreed answer yet — for those, the note matters more than the mark.

  1. C1 · Scan a barcode that isn't in the catalogue GAP G1 Banco says "Product with barcode 'x' not found" and the sale stops. What do you do next? Write the move you invent — that becomes the policy. This is not rare: it is every delivery since the last setup session.
  2. C2 · Scan a product that exists but is inactive GAP G1 "Product is inactive" and it stops. Same question, possibly a different answer.
  3. C3 · Screen price ≠ shelf price GAP G2 — sell at which one, and who is allowed to decide? no rule exists. Note what you'd tell Leandra to do. A wrong bind surfaces here, in front of a customer — Cannazym at CHF 12 instead of 35.
  4. C4 · Wrong item scanned — remove the line before checkout ⏱ < 15 s works today, no manager needed.
  5. C5 · Customer changes mind after paying GAP G6 — try it as the cashier. refund is manager-only and you will be blocked. Correct as a control, unworkable if Felix is out. Note what a cashier should actually do at 18:40 on a Saturday.
  6. C6 · Card declines — ask for another method. the basket is still open and takes the second method.
  7. C7 · Split payment — part cash, part card GAP G7 one method per transaction today. Note whether the shop actually needs split before go-live.
  8. C8 · "I forgot my wallet, back in ten minutes" GAP G5 no park-the-sale for a cashier. /pos/held-orders is the kiosk pre-order board, not a parked basket.
  9. C9 · Cash leaves the drawer (milk, window cleaner) — paid-out with a reason ⏱ < 30 s /pos/shift → paid-out works, and a reason is required.
  10. C10 · Cash comes in that isn't a sale (float top-up) — paid-in with a reason works, reason required.
  11. C11 · Gun goes flat mid-queue — swap the dongle to the charged one ⏱ < 30 s works — that is why there are two. Re-run the A2 hardware check after the swap.
  12. C12 · Customer offers foreign cash Banco tracks foreign cash per currency at close. This exists but has never been tested by a human — you are the first.
  13. C13 · 18+ item, no ID — refuse the line. no override exists, correctly.
Part D — Closing · target under 10 minutes

D · Count the drawer out ⏱ < 10 min

The paper version took 90 minutes. This is the step that has to feel worth it.

  1. D1 · Close the shift and count out, denomination by denomination. /pos/shift → close Banco computes expected = float + cash sales + paid-in − paid-out − cash refunds.
  2. D2 · Land inside tolerance — variance = counted − expected, tolerance CHF 0.20. closes clean.
  3. D3 · Now deliberately be out — count one note wrong on purpose. it will not close without a note. That is deliberate: the note is the audit trail. Check the wording makes sense to someone who is tired.
  4. D4 · Read the shift report — hours, transaction count, cash, card, expected, counted, variance. one page. Reload it at /pos/shift/last — closing must not lose it.
  5. D5 · Card sales are NOT in the drawer. Confirm the report makes that obvious. reported but not counted, and the screen says so. This is the classic first-week mistake — if the sheet doesn't make it obvious, that's a finding.
  6. D6 · Three closing screens GAP G8 — from the cashier's navigation, can you reach /pos/closeout, /pos/shift and /pos/cash-count? a cashier cannot have three closing screens and two meanings of "shift". Note which one you instinctively reached for — that is probably the real one.
Part E — The manager's part · after the cashiers have gone

E · Read the day and get it into Banana

Switch to a manager account for this part — and note the moment you had to.

  1. E1 · Read the day — totals, per payment method, VAT /pos/reports the numbers match what you actually rang up.
  2. E2 · Check each cashier's variance and read every note. your D3 note is there and readable.
  3. E3 · Process the refund you were blocked on in C5. works as manager. Time how long the whole cashier→manager round trip took.
  4. E4 · Export for Banana ⏱ < 5 min /api/v1/pos/reports/daily-summary.csv lands as banana-YYYY-MM-DD.csv: one quoted line per payment method that took money, a giveaway-cost line if treats went out, and a VAT summary block (8.1% / 2.6%) in its own Turnover and VAT columns.
  5. E5 · Actually import it into Banana. GAP G4 does it import? Account and VatCode ship blank — Felix maps them by hand every single day until he hands over his chart of accounts. Time the mapping. A daily manual step is what gets abandoned in week three, and it takes the bookkeeping with it.
Part F — The verdict

F · Did one person run the whole day alone?

  1. F1 · The whole cycle ran end to end, by one person, with no help — and the drawer balanced. that is the only definition of done. Anything else is a finding, and findings are the point.
  2. F2 · Could Leandra do this tomorrow with the till card alone? be honest. If the answer needs a "yes, but…", write the but.
