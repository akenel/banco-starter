Part C — The member paths · two ways to be 18, and they are not equal

C · Where the basis comes from

Four bases exist and they are split on purpose: not_required · member_dob · member_confirmed · cashier_attest. An inspector asking "how do you know she was 18" gets a very different answer for a date of birth than for a box someone once ticked — and you cannot fix a weakness you cannot count. All four are proven over HTTP by A5; here you are driving the screens a person actually uses.

  1. C1 · Enrol a member with a real date of birth (adult) and sell them the Gummy. clears with no attestation popup at all — the DOB is the answer. Records member_dob, the strongest basis.
  2. C2 · Enrol a member with NO date of birth, 18+ box ticked, and sell them the Gummy. also clears — member_confirmed. Correct, and deliberately counted apart from C1 because it rests on nothing but the tick.
  3. C3 · Enrol a member whose DOB makes them 15, then try the Gummy and tick the cashier attestation anyway. refused. A date of birth beats a cashier's word — there is no override and that is the point. This is the single most important behaviour on the sheet.
  4. C4 · Save a member with the contact boxes left blank. Not new work — this 500'd on 2026-08-03 and looked like the age gate. Re-check it here, because it is the same screen. saves. No 500.
Part D — The evidence · and the gap this run found

D · Can you show it to somebody? GAP

Read this before you mark anything. Every one of these steps is a database command, and that is not a convenience — it is the finding. Nothing in src/ reads any of it: grep for age_check_outcome, was_age_restricted, age_check_event or any compliance_* table and every single hit is a write. No endpoint, no template, no report, no screen.

This is the repo's most repeated bug, for the sixth time — after cash_box_float, the force-close, POST /catalog/merge, honest confidence, and best_match_score. Built on every layer a test can reach, and on no screen.
And it bites harder here than in any of those, because the entire purpose of this work is that a person can be shown the record. The seed SQL calls "records stored in editable folders" a standing nonconformity — records reachable only from a psql prompt are not obviously better. Felix cannot run these commands.
  1. D1 · Every sale you rang in Parts B and C carries its basis. docker compose exec -T postgres psql -U helix_user -d helix_db -c "select transaction_number, age_check_outcome, total from transactions order by created_at desc limit 10" your B1 shows not_required, B2 cashier_attest, C1 member_dob, C2 member_confirmed. Never blank — silence cannot tell "no 18+ line" from "nobody checked". Old sales from before today stay NULL, correctly: no historical sale gets retro-labelled with a check nobody performed.
  2. D2 · The refusals — the proof the gate actually bites. docker compose exec -T postgres psql -U helix_user -d helix_db -c "select occurred_at, outcome, product_class, cashier, note from age_check_event order by occurred_at desc limit 10" your B3 and C3 are both here. A shop with thousands of 18+ sales and zero refusals reads exactly like a shop that never checks — the refusal log is what makes the clearances believable.
  3. D3 · No buyer identity is stored. Look at what D2 printed. product class, cashier, timestamp — and nothing about the person turned away. FADP: the shop must prove the gate bit, not build a file on whoever it bit. Mark FAIL if you can see who anybody was.

This is the one that was silently broken: the first version used REVOKE, and a PostgreSQL table owner always keeps full rights on its own table, so the protection was a comforting no-op that tested green.   5.
