Section B · what changed today

Retest these four, they are all deployed

  1. A label that scans for goods with no manufacturer EAN. Three Crank pipes printed labels that could not be rung up. Scan a Crank pipe — small QR and medium barcode both.
  2. The gun sends SHIFT a beat late, so SKU-… arrives as sKU-…. Scan the shop's own label; case no longer matters.
  3. The PDF filename. Save a Small and a Medium label of the same product — they must not overwrite each other. Look for (sm) and (md).
  4. UPC-A ↔ EAN-13 padding. Section A above is the test.

Still unproven by a gun: the medium label's CODE128 on a 17-character SKU. If it will not read, the answer is a shorter SKU, not a bigger label.

Section D · not fixed, on purpose

Decisions waiting on a person

  • The classifier does not know the words “blunt” or “wrap.” It catches Swisher Sweets and misses Super Wrap Tropical. Teaching it is a one-line change and a compliance decision, so it is not made yet.
  • Adopting from the supplier copies the supplier's 18+ answer with no safety net, while the till's quick-add applies one. Same operation, two answers — that is how Tropical came in ungated.
  • Cuban Mojito and Lychee do not exist anywhere. They need creating, and cloning Blue is the cheap way — the clone endpoint is already built and is on no screen.
  • 58 active products priced CHF 999.99, including two identical JaJa Noir King Size XXL rows. Any of them can be sold at that price right now.
