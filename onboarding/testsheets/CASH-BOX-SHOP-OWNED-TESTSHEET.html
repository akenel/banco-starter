Part A — The morning · the blind count and the reveal

A · Open the box ⏱ < 3 min

Do these in order and do not skip A1 — once you have seen the expected figure you cannot un-see it, and A2 is worthless.

  1. A1 · Land on the open screen and READ IT before touching anything. /pos/shift it says “Count what is actually in the box” and shows no expected total, no ‘last night’, no float from yesterday — only the denomination grid. Any number on this screen before you count = FAIL.
  2. A2 · Put a count in the grid, denomination by denomination. At the shop: the real money. At your desk: invent one — see the box below. the running total at the bottom matches what you entered.
    Simulating? You do not need real money. What A1–A4 test is the order information arrives in, not coins: Banco must not tell you the answer before you commit to one. An invented count exercises that perfectly.
    Type 1×100 · 1×50 · 1×10 · 1×2 · 1×1 = CHF 163.00. It is picked to be ~5 short of the slope, so the reveal and the note requirement both fire (A3, A4) while staying close enough that the §6 guard stays quiet — you test that separately in Part B.
    The one rule that still applies: do not look up what the box should hold first. That is the whole control, money or no money.
  3. A3 · Submit. NOW the reveal appears. a blue panel says what last night's reconcile recorded, and by how much you differ. This is the first moment any expected figure exists on screen.
  4. A4 · If it differs, you cannot open without a note. Type what happened. the note is filed against yesterday, not against today — the wording should say so. Today's trading did not go wrong yet.
  5. A5 · Check what became today's float. /pos/shift → the OPEN panel the float is what you counted, not what was expected. Felix's rule: “I'm just gonna work with the five hundred I got and go from there.”
  6. A6 · The unverified warning SPECIFIC — right now the last reconcile on UAT is pam's box, force-closed at CHF 168.00 by me, which nobody ever counted. the reveal should warn you that figure was NEVER PHYSICALLY COUNTED — so the difference may be in that number rather than in the box. If it quotes 168.00 as plain fact, mark FAIL: that is the exact lie this feature exists to prevent.
Part B — The guard · it asks, it never refuses

B · The CHF 0.05 that started this

A shift really was opened on UAT with a float of five rappen, on a box that carries ~600, and nothing objected — because nothing had ever been told what normal looks like. Reconcile the box first if it is open, then run these.

  1. B1 · Set what the box normally holds. /pos/settings → Cash box float it saves. This is a policy (what it should carry), not a measurement.
  2. B2 · Open with something absurd — one 5-rappen coin. an amber panel: “The box should hold around CHF … You counted CHF 0.05. Is that right?” It must ASK, not refuse.
  3. B3 · Press “Let me recount”. back to the grid with your counts intact. Nothing was opened.
  4. B4 · Now press “Yes, that's what's in it”. it opens anyway. This matters: on the one morning the box really has been emptied, that is exactly the morning you want the shop open and the difference written down.
  5. B4b · Watch for a loop. After confirming, if it then asks for a note, type one and open. it opens. It must not bounce you back to “Is that right?” a second time — I found and fixed that loop, so this is the check that it stayed fixed.
  6. B5 · Reconcile, then open again with a normal count. no amber panel at all. The guard must not nag about ordinary variation.
Part C — TWO cashiers, ONE box · the headline bug

C · The whole reason for the rebuild needs 2 logins

Open a second browser or private window and log in as a different cashier. Everything here was either impossible or wrong before today.

  1. C1 · Felix opens the box. Pam does NOT open anything. Pam's /pos/shift shows the same open box, with “opened by felix”. It is not “her drawer” and there is nothing for her to open.
  2. C2 · Pam tries to open a second box. refused, and the message names who has it and when. Two people could previously hold open drawers on one physical box — that happened on prod today.
  3. C3 · Pam takes a CASH sale. WAS BROKEN /pos/scan → cash it goes through. Before today Pam could not take cash at all unless she had personally opened a drawer — on a box Felix had already opened.
  4. C4 · Felix rings a cash sale too. Then both of you look at the box. expected cash = float + BOTH your sales. Old behaviour: each of you saw only your own, so counting out reported the other's takings as missing.
  5. C5 · Check the sales report still splits by person. /pos/reports per-cashier sales are unchanged — who sold what still works. Only the blame for a variance is gone, and that is honesty, not a downgrade.
Part D — During the day

D · Reading the box without closing it

  1. D1 · The box is getting heavy — take a reading. /api/v1/pos/shift/x-report it reports the live position and changes nothing. Re-check /pos/shift after: still open, same numbers. This is the X-report — standard on registers for fifty years and missing from Banco until today. Note whether it needs a button — right now it is API-only.
  2. D2 · Skim CHF 500 to the safe. /pos/shift → paid out → reason “to safe” expected cash drops by 500. Check the reason choice: a skim is money leaving the drawer but not the business, so it must never book as petty cash next to milk and window cleaner. If the screen only gives you a free-text box, mark ISSUE and say so — the code supports to_safe, the screen may not offer it yet.
  3. D3 · A cashier does the paid-out, not a manager. no manager gate anywhere here. Your rule: the person holding the box is the person who counts it.

Part F — The forced close · and PUTTING THE BOX BACK

F · The one manager gate DO NOT SKIP F3

  1. F1 · As a cashier, try to force-close. POST /api/v1/pos/shift/force-close refused. This is the only manager-gated cash operation — it is not a correction a cashier needs, it is an admission that counting could not happen.
  2. F2 · As Felix, force-close with a real reason. it closes, variance 0.00 — and the report says counted_verified: false. A zero variance is exactly what a reader takes for “the drawer balanced”; the flag is what stops that lie. If the report shows a clean balance with no warning, mark FAIL.
  3. F3 · PUT THE BOX BACK. Open once more and reconcile at the number tomorrow should start from — the real contents at the shop, or if you are simulating, whatever you want the next morning's expected to be (write it in the note). the last reconcile now matches reality — so tomorrow morning starts from a true number instead of your test. This step is the difference between a test and a mess.
