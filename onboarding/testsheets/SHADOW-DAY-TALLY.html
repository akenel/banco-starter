The shop risks nothing. Felix and the girls change nothing and type nothing. The paper book is still the record of the day. Banco is a silent second copy that Angel keys in, standing to one side.

Ring it as it happens — never rebuild the day at 18:00. Reconstructing from the sheet at closing would prove the totals and hide every single thing that went wrong at the counter, and those are the findings.

This day is also the department-key dress rehearsal, done on paper. Every item that will not scan gets a button letter in the KEY column. At close you will know the real percentage and the real spread across the ten buttons — before a line of code is written. If a bucket nobody predicted keeps coming up, that is the tenth button.

Before you start — do not skip these, they cost one hour and save the day

  1. Ring the paper book's 477.– day on the tablet, alone, first. If Banco lands on 477 the arithmetic is proved and any defect you hit cost you nothing instead of costing you Felix's patience.
  2. Till guard + cashier price panel touched by a human (WORKLIST item A). It sits between the shop and every sale and no person has used it yet.
  3. Cash box float set on a screen — not by curl.
  4. Decide the loose cigarette (Zigi einzeln 1.–) before the day, not at the counter with a queue behind you.
  5. Check the fridge drinks exist in the catalogue at all — 46 items in Cafe & Food and only 1 of them scans.
  6. Phone charged, paper sheet and pen beside the tablet. You cannot tally on the same device you are ringing on.
🔴 The demo realm. A shadow day puts real sales on an instance whose passwords are in a public GitHub repo. Close it first, or run the day on a local instance — and do not let the shadow run quietly become go-live.
