A · Rounding — the feature that has never once fired

🪙 A1 · Why this is not a formality never tested in prod

Shipped 2026-08-03 with 35 unit tests and a live proof. In real trading it has produced zero rounding adjustments — rounding_adjustment is 0.00 on all 8 of this morning's sales.

And now we know why. Artemis prices end in 0 or 5 — 5.90, 6.95, 49.90, 40.00. Any sum of those is already a multiple of 5 rappen, so there is nothing to round. The engine may almost never fire in normal trading, which makes the rare time it does the time nobody will be expecting it.

The one real product that breaks the pattern: Rips Hemp King Size rolling papers 5m — unit price CHF 1.67. One of them is an uneven total. That is our test.

🪙 A2 · One Rips, paid in cash

The smallest possible uneven total. 1.67 cannot be handed over — there is no 1- or 2-rappen coin in Switzerland.

  1. Ring 1 × Rips Hemp King Size and nothing else. Look at the cart subtotal before choosing a payment method. 1.67
  2. Choose CASH. Read the total the screen now asks for. 1.65 — and it says somewhere that it was rounded
  3. Tender 2.00. Check the change. 0.35, not 0.33
  4. Look at the printed/on-screen receipt. Does it show the rounding as its own line? A total that silently differs from the sum of the items is how a customer loses trust in a till. a visible rounding line, not a mystery 2 rappen

🪙 A3 · The same cart on TWINT — the control

This is the check that matters, and it is the one a script cannot fake. Do not work out the expected number yourself. On 2026-08-03 a proof script recomputed the answer, got it wrong, and produced two ❌s against code that was right. Ring the identical cart on a card and let the system hand you the authoritative figure.

  1. Ring 1 × Rips again. Pay by TWINT. 1.67 — cards do not round, only coins have the constraint
  2. Put the two side by side: same cart, two payment methods, differing by exactly the rounding. 1.67 vs 1.65 — a 0.02 difference and nothing else
  3. Ring 2 × Rips in cash (3.34). 3.35 — rounds UP. Nearest, not always down.
  4. Now check the drawer: X-report. Does expected cash account for the rounding, or is it quietly 2 rappen out? expected matches what you would physically count
B · The drawer that disagrees — the whole point of a cash box

⚖️ B1 · Count it SHORT never tested

Three closes this morning, three variances of exactly 0.00. The screen has never once had to say "this does not match". A cash box that only handles agreement is not a cash box.

Do not look up what it should be before you count. Type a number that is deliberately wrong first, and see what the screen does with it.
  1. Close the shift. When it asks for the count, enter 10.00 less than you believe is there. it says SHORT, shows the gap, and does NOT show a green tick
  2. Is the number it shows the gap, or does it just tell you what it expected? Telling you the expected figure teaches you to type it next time. the variance, not a hint
  3. Does it demand a note before letting you finish? a short cash box that closes silently is money quietly gone
  4. Write a real note — "miscounted, recounting" — and try to go back and correct the count without closing. a way back. A wrong number you cannot fix is a wrong number in the books.

⚖️ B2 · Count it OVER, and count it exact

  1. Enter 10.00 more than expected. OVER is called out just as loudly as SHORT
  2. Now enter the right figure and finish the close. balanced — and NOW the green tick is honest
  3. 🔴 The one that shipped broken before. Check the green "✅ Balanced" is not rendering over a box you have not counted yet. On 2026-08-03 it did exactly that — the largest element on the page, directly above the note explaining it was not true. no green anything until a count has been entered
  4. Open the next shift. Does the float carry from what you just counted? the new float = your counted figure, to the rappen
C · Money out of the box — and the reason code that has never held a value

💸 C1 · Take CHF 500 to the safe reason_code always empty

Two cash movements exist in the whole database, both from 2026-08-03, and reason_code is empty on both — they predate the named reasons. So the feature built on 2026-08-03 has never once stored a value.

The bug this replaced: a CHF 500 skim never saved. The cashier chose the movement, typed the amount, pressed Record — and a 400 landed in a banner at the top of a page he had scrolled past. Nothing saved, nothing looked wrong, and the report simply showed no paid-out. Watch where the outcome appears, not just whether it worked.
  1. Record a paid-out of 500.00, reason to the safe from the dropdown. Leave any free-text box empty. it saves. "to the safe" IS the reason — no sentence required after it.
  2. 🔴 Where did the confirmation appear? Scroll position matters — stand where the button is and see if you can tell it worked without scrolling. the outcome appears where the button is
  3. Record a paid-in of 100.00, reason float top-up. saves, and the expected cash moves by +100
  4. X-report: are paid-in and paid-out shown separately, with their reasons, or blended into one number? both visible with their reasons — you can see where money went
  5. Close and check the expected figure includes both movements. float + cash sales − 500 + 100
D · Undoing a sale — a customer changed their mind

↩️ D1 · Refund and void all 8 sales COMPLETED, nothing undone

Every transaction in the database is COMPLETED. Nothing has ever been refunded, voided or abandoned. In a real shop this happens on day one.

  1. Ring a small cash sale, complete it, then refund it. cash_refunds moves, and expected cash comes back DOWN
  2. Does the refund show on the X-report as its own figure, not just netted into sales? refunds visible separately — netting hides them
  3. Start a cart, add three items, then abandon it — walk away, hit back, close the tab. no phantom transaction, and the drawer is unaffected
  4. ⚠️ Then reload the page. Is the abandoned cart still there? The cart lives in sessionStorage, so it dies with the tab — and it does not travel to the other tablet. know which it is, and tell the cashier
  5. Refund a card sale. Does Banco make clear it has recorded a refund and not moved any money — the card refund is Worldline's job? no implication that the customer has been paid back
