# Shelf wall — 2026-08-06 (Artemis Luzern)

42 photos taken off the accessory wall and the glass counter. Filed by class,
renamed to describe what is *in* the frame. The trailing `-wNN` is the original
capture number so nothing loses its place in the roll.

**How to use this file:** `grep` it. The descriptions carry the words you'd
actually type — brand, form, material, colour, price sticker.

## What this roll is worth

Three things came out of it that nothing in the database knew:

1. **`signs/sign-papers-bundle-prices-full-w03.jpg`** — the shop's own bundle
   price sign. `3x Longpape 5.-` / `3x Rollenpape 10.-` / `Box Longpape 39.-` /
   `Box Rollen 49.-`, all manufacturers. This closes the 30-July worklist item
   on paper tier pricing. Most valuable photo of the session.
2. **Price stickers already exist on the stock Felix said had no barcodes** —
   `25.-` on bongs, `10.-` on wooden grinders, `1.-` on glass bowls, `59.-` on
   a novelty pipe, `29.-` on a Luzern pouch. The objection is to **barcodes**,
   not to labelling. That changes what we build.
3. **`signs/sign-poettli-zwischenstuecke-glass-adapters-w15.jpg`** — a jar of
   loose bowls and adapters sold by the piece at `1.-`. This is the bong-parts
   compatibility problem sitting in a glass on the counter.
