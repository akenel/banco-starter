5 · Decisions, not defects

tomorrow
One controlled sale on prod, refunded immediately
Nothing has ever completed a sale on this build. Every test all week stops at the payment button, deliberately, because a completed transaction is a line in the Kassenbücher. Without this, Layla's shift is the first real sale this code has rung — with a customer standing there. Agreed for tomorrow. It leaves two entries in the books that Felix should be told about beforehand, not after.
parked
Birth year instead of a full birthdate
Faster, more private, and the shop already works that way — of 22 active members, 4 have a birthdate, 3 have a cashier attestation and no birthdate, 15 have neither. Held because a year cannot decide age for one cohort (born 2008 is 18 in January and 17 in December) and because working compliance code should not be touched for a hypothetical. Ask Felix only if he raises it.
