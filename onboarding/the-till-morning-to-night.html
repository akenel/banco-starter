Banco POS · the shop tablet

The till, morning to night

One page. Pin it by the till. Everything here is something the tablet really does — it was all tested on this machine on 5 September 2026.

Opening first thing

  1. Press the power button once.
  2. Wait about twenty seconds. The till comes up on its own — no password, nothing to tap.
  3. If the screen looks dim, touch it and it brightens.
You do not have to do anything about updates. The tablet installs its own security updates overnight, or shortly after you switch it on if it was off. It never restarts itself while the shop is open.

During the day what is normal

  • Leave it plugged in. That is the only rule that matters.
  • The screen goes dim when nobody touches it. That is normal and it is not asleep — one touch brings it back. It will never go fully black on its own.
  • If the screen is black, the tablet is genuinely off. Press the power button.
Never press the power button to wake it. If the screen is dim, touch the glass. The power button is only for turning it on in the morning and off at night.

If the till ends up half off the screen it happens — it is fixable

Usually because a finger brushed the grey bar at the very top. Nothing is broken and nothing is lost. Any one of these puts it back:

Do thisWhat happens
Put a finger on the grey bar at the top and drag it back to the middle Always works, even if something is open on the screen
Tap the orange ⛶ Tap to fill the screenStraight back to full size
Tap ⛶ next to itSame thing
Do not restart the tablet for this. It takes one drag or one tap, and restarting in front of a customer costs you a minute you do not have.

Closing end of day

  1. Do the close-out on the till as usual — count the cash, close the shift.
  2. You can unplug it and carry it to wherever you count. It will not go to sleep or lock itself while you work, on battery or not.
  3. When you are finished, plug it back into the cradle.
  4. Hold the power button until the screen asks. Choose Power Off.
Turning it off at night is fine — it catches up on its updates in the morning. Leaving it on is fine too. Both work.

Please don't three things

  • Don't press the × in the top right corner. If you do by accident, the till comes back by itself after a few seconds. If it doesn't, press Banco again from Activities, top-left.
  • Don't use the till's browser to look things up on the web. Use Firefox for that — it is on the machine and it is what it is there for. Opening the web in the till's own window is how you end up with two tills open at once.
  • Don't leave it unplugged overnight. Unplugged is for counting, not for sleeping.

If any of this feels clunky say so

This tablet runs Linux, not Android, and some of it behaves like a computer rather than a phone. That is a choice we can revisit. If the window moving about is annoying you more than once a week, say so — there is a stricter mode that locks the till to the whole screen. It costs you the battery indicator and the settings, so we have not done it. Your call, once you have lived with it.
